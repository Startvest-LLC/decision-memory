# Rejection Rationale Template

Use when saying no to a request. Honest, constructive, professional.

## Template

```markdown
## Decision: Not Building [Feature Name]

**To**: [Requester/Stakeholders]
**Date**: [YYYY-MM-DD]

### The Request
[What was asked for - acknowledge it clearly]

### Our Decision
We've decided not to build this [right now / at all].

### Why
[Honest, specific reasoning - not corporate speak]

### What We're Doing Instead
[Alternative, workaround, or nothing - be clear]

### Revisit
[When/if we might reconsider, or "This is final"]

---

We appreciate the feedback and encourage you to keep sharing ideas.
Not every idea becomes a feature, but every idea helps us understand our users better.
```

## Example: Filled Out

```markdown
## Decision: Not Building Excel Export

**To**: Enterprise customers who requested
**Date**: 2025-01-27

### The Request
Native Excel export from the analytics dashboard with custom formatting.

### Our Decision
We've decided not to build this right now.

### Why
Two reasons:
1. **Low demand**: Only 3 of 200 enterprise accounts requested this.
2. **Workaround exists**: CSV export works with Excel, and our Zapier integration can automate Excel formatting.

### What We're Doing Instead
We're improving CSV export with more column options and scheduled exports.

### Revisit
If 10+ enterprise accounts request native Excel export, we'll reprioritize.

---

We appreciate the feedback! The CSV improvements coming in Q2 should cover most use cases.
```

## Dos and Don'ts

### Do
- Be honest about the real reasons
- Acknowledge their request respectfully
- Offer alternatives if they exist
- Set clear revisit conditions

### Don't
- Say "not a priority" (what does that mean?)
- Blame resources without specifics
- Promise to "consider it later" vaguely
- Ignore the request entirely
